How Your Deck Is Crafted

1. Choose a Commander & Game Plan

Every deck starts with a clear identity: tempo, value, tribal, or synergy‑based strategies.

2. Build for Synergy

Cards are chosen to work together—curve, interaction, threats, and finishers all support the same plan.

3. Tune the Mana Base

Lands and ramp are selected to keep games smooth and playable.

4. Final Review & Packaging

Lists are checked for consistency, then sleeved and packaged as ready‑to‑play 100‑card decks.
