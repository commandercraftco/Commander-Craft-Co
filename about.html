About Me

Hi, my name is Ian. I’m from Nevada, and I started playing Magic: The Gathering a little over a year ago. Magic quickly became one of my favorite hobbies—I love learning new strategies, building decks, and playing games with friends. Over time, I realized I also really enjoy crafting decks that feel unique, fun, and strong at the table. Now I’m turning that hobby into something bigger by creating decks for players who want a deck that’s thoughtful, consistent, and ready to win real games.
